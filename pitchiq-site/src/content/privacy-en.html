This policy explains how the Pitch IQ mobile app and web version (the "App") handle information. In short: we do not collect personal data and we do not use ads or tracking tools.

1. Information we collect

The App does not ask you to create an account or provide personal information such as your email, phone number, location or contacts.

  • Data kept on your device: Your nickname, chosen team, game progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Live leaderboard (optional): If you turn on the live leaderboard, only your nickname and score are sent to our leaderboard server and shown to other players. If you turn it off, nothing is sent.
  • Purchases: Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We have no access to your payment details; Google only tells us whether a subscription is active. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Ads, analytics and tracking

The App contains no ads, no advertising ID (AAID), and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

3. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard nicknames and scores are kept while the feature is enabled. To have them deleted, email {{email}} with your nickname and we will remove them within 30 days. To delete on-device data, uninstall the App or clear its app data.

4. Security

All communication with the leaderboard server uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. We recommend not using your real name as a nickname.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies. If visits are measured, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics tool that stores no personal data is used. Your theme preference is stored only in your browser.

7. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

To exercise your rights of access, correction and deletion, contact us by email.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date.
